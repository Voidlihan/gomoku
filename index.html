How to play?

GoMoKu is a strategic board game where two players take turns placing their pieces on a 10x10 grid. The objective is to be the first to align five of your pieces in a row, either horizontally (----), vertically (|), or diagonally (/, \). Players must carefully plan their moves to block their opponent while trying to create their own winning line. The game requires foresight and tactical thinking, making it a fun and challenging experience for players of all ages.
